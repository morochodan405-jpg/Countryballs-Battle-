HOW TO PLAY

Everything you need to survive the battlefield.

Goal

Pick a countryball and battle through 35 waves of invading balls to win the war. Each wave gets tougher. Survive the zombie infection on waves 15–20 (strong zombie balls!), then push on to wave 35 for victory. Earn coins and XP to level up (1–100) and unlock better gear.

Controls

  • W A S D / Arrows — move your ball
  • Mouse — aim (your eyes follow the cursor)
  • Auto-fire is on by default — or hold Click / Space
  • T — toggle auto-fire on/off (stop shooting)
  • F — swing your sword (melee, no ammo)
  • C — swing your axe to chop trees for wood coins (also hits enemies)
  • B — build defensive blocks (costs coins). You can still shoot while building.
  • K — if the game is lagging, restart the bots to clear it (does nothing when there's no lag)
  • P — pause
  • iPad Mode — on-screen joystick to move, touch the map to aim/shoot, and buttons for Shoot, Sword, Axe, Build, Auto-fire, Lag Fix and Pause

Melee & Chopping Trees

  • F — swing your sword for close-range melee (no ammo needed).
  • C — swing your axe to chop trees. Each felled tree drops wood coins (boosted by your Fortune power), so trees are a handy way to farm coins.
  • The axe also hits enemies in an arc — a solid backup when you're out of ammo.
  • Trees show a green health bar while you chop and shake with each hit.

Survival

  • Watch your Health, Food and Water bars — grab pickups dropped by enemies or found on the grass.
  • If your Food or Water hits 0, you get a 3·2·1·0 countdown — eat or drink before it hits 0 or you collapse and die.
  • Ammo is limited. Out of ammo on snow? You throw snowballs instead.
  • Buy a coat in the Gear Shop or you'll freeze on snow. Deserts drain water fast.

Sea & Land Dangers

  • Step onto water to sail a boat (3·2·1·0 to set off). Reach land to walk again.
  • Icebergs sink your boat — swim to shore before the countdown ends!
  • Sharks bite your boat, bears grab you on land (shoot them to escape).
  • After wave 10: a flood hits — only hills stay dry.

Shops

  • Gun Shop, Boat Yard, Power Lab, Gear Shop, Cosmetics — spend coins to grow stronger. Progress saves automatically.

Laggy? Try this

  • During a lag spike, press K to restart the bots (only works when the FPS is actually low).
  • Turn on Low Graphics (button on the start/pause screen).
  • Make the browser window smaller (don't run full 4K).
  • Enable hardware acceleration in your browser settings.
